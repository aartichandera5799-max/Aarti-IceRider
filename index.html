01 / THE COMPANY

Care for the cargo
that can’t wait.

IceRider Logistics is a class-project cold-chain business concept for temperature-sensitive deliveries. We bring shipment status, temperature updates, and incident communications into one clear operations view.

Our sample workflows are designed for pharma, vaccine, food, and other sensitive-cargo teams, with illustrative lanes connecting Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, and Nashik. Demo figures and routes do not represent a live carrier network.
